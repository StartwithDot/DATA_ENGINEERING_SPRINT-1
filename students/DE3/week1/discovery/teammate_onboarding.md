## OVERVIEW :
 Our client is a financial due diligence company whose primary customers are organizations such as banks and other financial institutions that lend money to businesses and companies. The main objective of our product is to build a single centralized platform/dashboard where the client can view and analyze all the important information about a company before making a lending decision.

## dataset:

 1. MCA
   The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) provides key information about registered companies through their unique Corporate Identification Number (CIN). The platform should collect information such as:

 - Company Name
 - CIN
 - Registration Date
 - Current Status

 2. Insolvency Information — IBBI
   We also need to identify whether the company is currently involved in or has previously gone through insolvency proceedings. This information can be obtained from the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI).

 3. Sector/Industry Analysis
   Another important component is understanding the performance and activity of the sector in which the company operates.

 4. RBI Repo Rate
   Knowing the repo rate at the exact time a company was registered gives lenders a quick snapshot of the economic condition — whether money was expensive or cheap when that business first opened its doors.

## Relevant historical changes

 An important requirement is that the dashboard should not simply overwrite existing data when new information arrives. The system should:

 - Regularly update company information
 - Maintain historical records
 - Track changes in company status

 This will allow the client to understand not only the current position of a company but also how its position has changed over time.

## End Goal

 The ultimate goal is to create a centralized financial due-diligence platform that brings together company information, insolvency records, sector-level information, and economic indicators into one place.
