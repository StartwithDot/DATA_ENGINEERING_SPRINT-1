
# MCA Company Master Data

MCA data contains company names, CINs, and registration status.
It helps us check whether a company is registered and active.
It comes as CSV files inside ZIP files, updated roughly monthly.

# IBBI Insolvency Records

IBBI data contains records of companies involved in insolvency cases.
It helps us check whether a company has faced insolvency.
It comes as PDF files, updated roughly quarterly, which we convert to CSV.

# MCA Corporate Data Management Portal

MCA CDM provides statistics about companies across Indian states.
It helps us compare a company's state with other states.
It comes as HTML tables on web pages, which we collect using Python.

# RBI Policy Rate Data

RBI data contains historical policy interest rates.
It helps us understand interest-rate conditions during a company's history.
It comes as downloadable Excel or CSV files, and our downloaded data ends in 2024.

Note: The RBI DBIE portal is now available at https://data.rbi.org.in. Policy rate data lists only the dates when rates changed, so a missing month does not mean data is missing. RBI data is background information only.
