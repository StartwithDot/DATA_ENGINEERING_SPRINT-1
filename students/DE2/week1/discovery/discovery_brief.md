
## What we are building
A data platform to check Indian companies.

## Who uses it
Fintech analysts who check companies before investments or deals.

## Questions it must answer
Is the company active?,Does it have an insolvency history?,How does its state compare?,What were the interest rates?

## What is still unclear
nothing is unclear - only the architecture

## What "done" looks like
A working complete end to end data platform and companies can filter out any company names and can find its history to  arrive at any decision whether to invest in the company or not in the Metabase dashboard.
