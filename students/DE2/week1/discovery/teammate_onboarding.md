 # 1
 Our client is a DUE_DILEGENCE company that checks Indian companies before  investments or business deals. 
 # 2
 We are building a metabase data platform using Snowflake.
 We use MCA company data
 IBBI insolvency records
 MCA state statistics
 RBI interest rate data. 
 # 3
 These sources help us check company status, insolvency history, and state level comparisons. RBI data provides interest rate background. The platform organizes data into bronze, silver, and gold layers. 
 # 4
 The finished product includes reliable data tables and a Metabase dashboard. Analysts can search for a company, check its history, identify insolvency records, and compare it with its state and industry.
