## What we are building
As our client is a fintech due diligence company we are building a reliable due diligence platform to simply monitor different companies current status,history and finacial risks incorprated by collecting and transforming different real data sources.

## Who uses it
Company wanted a dashboard  that a non technical person can use for find 
all registered company,insolvency events attached to the right companies ,state level context for lending ,buying and signing contracts

## Questions it must answer
First, is this company really registered and still active. Second, has this company ever been in financial trouble, like bankruptcy proceedings. Third, how does this company compare to others in its state and industry.

## What is still unclear
They want the whole thing to refresh on a regular schedule as i noticed the data sources and new data updation is on uncertain times and may the data file names wouldn't follow same pattern so auto extraction will needed a through check before extraction.

## What "done" looks like.
An automated single dashboard that refreshes daily for daily analysts , lenders, banks, and procurement teams for do finacial due diligence on companies through a user-friendly dashboard that a non technical person can use.
