Summary of Four Data Sources

1. MCA Company Master Data

MCA Company Master Data contains details of companies registered in India, such as their company name, CIN number, registration date, and current status. We use this data to check whether a company is registered and whether it is still active or has been closed. The data is available on data.gov.in as ZIP files containing CSV files, and new data is usually released every month.

2. IBBI Insolvency Records

IBBI provides information about companies that have gone through insolvency proceedings, which happen when a company faces serious financial problems and cannot pay its debts. We use this data to check whether a company has been involved in insolvency proceedings before. The information is published as PDF files, usually every three months, and we need to extract the details into a usable format.

3. MCA Corporate Data Management Portal

The MCA Corporate Data Management portal provides information about companies across different states and industries in India. We use this data to understand how a company compares with other businesses in its state and industry. The information is available in tables on the website, and we need to collect it from the web pages because it is not provided as a direct downloadable data file.

4. RBI Policy Rate Data

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) provides information about interest rates, including the repo rate, through its Database on the Indian Economy (DBIE) portal. We use this data to understand the interest rate at a particular time, such as when a company was registered, but it is only for background information and not for checking a company's risk directly. The data can be downloaded as CSV or Excel files, and the policy rate data records only the dates when the rate changes.
