1. 
Our client is a fintech company that helps banks, lenders, and other businesses check a company before working with it.

2. 
We are building a platform that brings all the important company details into one place. 

3. 
We use MCA data to check whether a company is registered and what its current status is.
We use IBBI data to find out whether a company has faced insolvency problems. 
The MCA Corporate Data Management portal helps us compare companies with other businesses in the same state and industry. 
We also use RBI data to understand interest rates during a particular period. 


Since the data comes in different formats, such as ZIP, CSV, PDF, Excel files, and website tables, we need to organise and clean it before using it. 
Our data pipeline will update the information regularly, check for errors, and keep track of changes over time. 

4. 
In the end, we will create a Metabase dashboard where analysts can search for a company, check its current and past status, see its insolvency records, and compare it with other businesses.
