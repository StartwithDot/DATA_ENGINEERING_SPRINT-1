We are building a data platform for a due deligence company their primary aim is to give a clear idea for their customers by looking at our platform so the main customers of the due deligence company are lenders, banks, and procurement teams they need to ensure that the background is clear for every companies that they are dealing with

 The due deligence company need to know 3 things from our platform first, is this company really registered and still active Second, has this company ever been in financial trouble like bankruptcy proceedings. Third, how does this company compare to others in its state and industry.

For this we are mainly taking datas from 4 sources

1.MCA company master data:MCA stands for ministry of corporate affairs it is the government body that registers every company in india.it assign a 21 character unique number to every company that registers called CIN.For each companyit will provide the CIN, the company name, the registration date, the current statu which answers the question first question

2.IBBI Insolvency Records-IBBI stands for Bankruptcy Board of India it look after the bankruptcy proceedings when a company cannot pay their debt they will go through a process called Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process IBBI published the names of companies going through such process which answers the second question

3.MCA Corporate Data Management Portal:It is a public analylitcs portal in which it has the datas of the active and inactive companies,the details of differents sectors performing, the comparison with public and private sector,also have the state wise performance which answers the last question

4.RBI Policy Rate Data:which decides the repo rate,which is interest rate at which the central bank lends money to the commercial bank Repo rate is not direct but an indirect tool for calculating how the company has raised so far

The dataset of all these are in different file format we need to convert it into a suitable structure and there also some cleaning process should be also done with the data set

The final done will look like if a non technical person look at the platform and search for a company it should give the idea of how the company is ,lending or buying from that company is good or bad decision not by just a random prediction by citing the proofs that are supporting the statement
