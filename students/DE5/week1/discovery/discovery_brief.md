## What we are building, 
We are building a tool that brings company details from four government websites into one place. Right now, workers search each site by hand and paste numbers into Excel. Our tool will collect the data automatically and show it on a simple screen.

## Who uses it, 
Bank workers, lenders, and buyers use this tool before giving loans or signing deals with a company. 
They are normal business people, not tech experts, so the screen must be easy to read and use.

## Questions it must answer,
Is this company real, and is it still active today?
Did this company ever face bankruptcy or fail to pay its debts?
How does this company compare to other companies in its state?
What was the bank interest rate when this company started?

 ## What is still unclear, 
nothing is unclear the architecture
 
 ## What "done" looks like.
Done means the system updates itself regularly without manual work. A user can search any company name and quickly see a clear, reliable report to make safe business decisions.
