1. Who the client is

Our client is a fintech due diligence company. Their customers are banks, financial lenders, and procurement teams who need to thoroughly check a business before lending it money or signing contracts.

2. What we build

We are building a centralized data platform using Python and Snowflake, connected to an easy Metabase dashboard. It automatically collects and cleans company records on a regular schedule, eliminating the need for analysts to search websites and copy numbers into Excel by hand.

3. Which sources we use

We use four Indian government websites:

MCA Company Master Data (data.gov.in): Official registry of company names, CIN numbers, and active/closed status.

IBBI Insolvency Records (ibbi.gov.in): Bankruptcy court records showing companies that failed to pay their debts.

MCA Data Portal (mcacdm.nic.in): State and industry statistics to see how many active companies exist in each sector.

RBI Policy Rate Data (data.rbi.org.in): Central bank repo interest rates to provide economic borrowing context.


4. What the finished thing answers

Is this company real, legally registered, and still active today?
Has this company ever faced serious financial trouble or bankruptcy court?
How does this company compare in size and capital to competitors in the same state?
What was the economic lending rate when the company first opened its doors?
