1---

Our client is a fintech due diligence company. Their customers are banks, financial lenders, and procurement teams who need to thoroughly check a business before lending it money or signing contracts.

2---

We are building a centralized data platform using Python and Snowflake, connected to an easy Metabase dashboard. It automatically collects and cleans company records on a regular schedule, eliminating the need for analysts to search websites and copy numbers into Excel by hand.

3---

MCA Company Master Data: Official registry of company names, CIN numbers, and active/closed status.

IBBI Insolvency Records: Bankruptcy court records showing companies that failed to pay their debts.

MCA Data Portal: State and industry statistics to see how many active companies exist in each sector.

RBI Policy Rate Data: Central bank repo interest rates to provide economic borrowing context.


4---

The final done will look like if a non technical person look at the platform and search for a company it should give the idea of how the company is ,lending or buying from that company is good or bad decision not by just a random prediction by citing the proofs that are supporting the statement
